KGVerse AutoClipper

A personal video-editing tool that runs only on its owner's own computer.

KGVerse AutoClipper turns long videos into short vertical clips (captions, framing, loudness) for the owner's own social media accounts, including the TikTok account @clipzone886. It is not a public service: there are no other users, no sign-ups and no hosted servers. It only connects to accounts its owner signs in to.

On TikTok it uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, only for the owner's own account, and only after the owner authorizes it.
